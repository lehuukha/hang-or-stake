## Dan Durston (Kakwa maker) on 40 vs 55, quoted by the hiker
"The 55L outsells the 40L about 3:1. The main upside of the 40L is that it is a bit lighter. Other than that, the 55L can roll down to be plenty small if you want, so it can do everything the 40L can plus more. If you're reliably at or below the 40L mark then get the 40L, whereas if you're not sure or all over the place (e.g. bulkier sometimes on long trips or winter trips) then the 55L has that covered."
Note: for the Hyperlite pair the weight difference is within ~1 oz, so his "main upside of the 40" mostly does not apply.
