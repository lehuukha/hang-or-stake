## Decisions made
- 2026-09-04: Keep the Hyperlite Southwest 40 (2025 woven, M). Return the Southwest 55. Pack test: all three quilts (Burrow 40, Phoenix 40, Superior Gear 20F full-length UQ), three days of food, Cerium LT, in a Nylofume liner, closed with four to five roll-top folds. Winter or 5-plus-day trips get a separate pack later (Durston Kakwa 55 for shoulder-season multi-day; Southwest 70 for winter).
- 2026-09-04: Pillow is now a Sea to Summit Aeros Ultralight (regular), 2.1 oz mfr spec. The Hammock Gear down pillow was shipped to GearTrade by mistake inside a returned Phoenix 40's loft sack; recovery email sent.
- 2026-09-04: Phoenix 40 3/4 on the list is now a friend's custom one, weight not yet measured; list still carries the 11 oz builder spec.
- Tent path: a DCF solo tent for sites where hanging isn't possible. Shortlist: used Zpacks Pivot Solo Standard ($500 asked on eBay, counter at $450), Durston X-Mid Pro 2 ($679) if room matters more than footprint, Durston X-Dome 1+ ($359 silpoly) if freestanding matters. Pivot needs 8 stakes and a rear pole that collapses to 32 in (or Zpacks' 32 in carbon pole).
- Pad to pair with the tent: insulated inflatable, R-value 4 or higher (NeoAir XLite NXT or Nemo Tensor All-Season class), wide.
