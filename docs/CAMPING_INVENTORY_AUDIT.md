# Camping inventory audit, September 6, 2026

Counts refer to individual backpack, walk-in and cart-in sites. Group, drive-in, bicycle-only, paddle-access and dispersed camping are separate. Inventory is not current vacancy. Official maps were visually inspected where text extraction omitted site symbols.

## Afton

**28 backpack / walk-in sites.** 27 main backpack sites + 1 separate walk-in site. Two group camps are separate. The separate site is accessible on foot or from the river. Inventory is not nightly availability.

Evidence: 27 backpack sites available year-round

[DNR camping inventory](https://maps.dnr.state.mn.us/cgi-bin/compass/feature_detail.cgi?id=spk00100)

## Lake Maria

**17 backpack sites.** 17 backpack sites + 2 separate group camps. DNR alert lists B1 closed as of August 22, 2026. Seventeen is the park inventory, not the number currently open or available.

Evidence: 17 Sites

[DNR inventory and closure alert](https://maps.dnr.state.mn.us/cgi-bin/compass/feature_detail.cgi?id=spk00217)

## Sand Dunes SF

**6 mapped walk-in sites.** Ann Lake sites 31–36 are the six mapped walk-ins. Dispersed camping has no fixed site count. The DNR campground map and 2017 forest appendix support six walk-ins. The current forest overview does not repeat that breakdown; confirm the operational count with Lake Maria State Park.

Evidence: 6 walk-in sites

[DNR Ann Lake campground map](https://files.dnr.state.mn.us/maps/state_forests/cmp00046.pdf), [DNR forest appendix (2017)](https://files.dnr.state.mn.us/forestry/subsection/anoka/sfrmp_anoka-appendices.pdf), [Current DNR forest information](https://maps.dnr.state.mn.us/cgi-bin/compass/feature_detail.cgi?id=sft00045)

## Cleary Lake Regional

**14 walk-in sites.** Basswood: 14 walk-in sites. White Pine: 6 drive-in sites. Two RV sites and 3 group camps are separate. The page introduction says 25 tent sites, but its detailed breakdown and April 29, 2026 map identify 14 Basswood walk-ins. Do not use the headline as a walk-in count.

Evidence: Basswood has 14 camp sites and two RV sites.

[Three Rivers campground details](https://www.threeriversparks.org/location/cleary-lake-campground), [April 2026 campground map](https://trpd.maps.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/b70154da024f4cc89f18e4822e261e40/data)

## Nerstrand Big Woods

**4 walk-in + 6 cart-in sites.** 4 walk-in sites + 6 cart-in sites = 10 sites reached on foot. The earlier guide omitted the cart-in category. These are separate from the drive-in campground.

Evidence: Six rustic sites, located 150 to 200 feet from parking.

[DNR camping inventory](https://maps.dnr.state.mn.us/cgi-bin/compass/feature_detail.cgi?id=spk00241)

## Wild River

**8 backpack sites.** Aspen Knob, Breezy Valley, Buck Hill, Deer Creek, Dry Creek Hollow, Meadow Vista, Pine Ridge and Spring Creek. Four canoe-in sites and nine walk-to group camps are separate categories. The guide previously described only six backpack sites.

Evidence: There are eight backpack sites.

[DNR camping inventory](https://maps.dnr.state.mn.us/cgi-bin/compass/feature_detail.cgi?id=spk00254), [DNR 2025 map](https://files.dnr.state.mn.us/maps/state_parks/spk00254.pdf)

## Frontenac

**2 backpack + 6 cart-in sites.** 2 backpack sites + 6 cart-in sites = 8 sites reached on foot. The guide previously had only two individual cart-in descriptions and one entry covering both backpack sites. DNR says neither backpack site is good for hammock camping.

Evidence: Two backpack sites located one and one-quarter miles from the designated parking lot at the ranger station.

[DNR camping inventory](https://maps.dnr.state.mn.us/cgi-bin/compass/feature_detail.cgi?id=spk00160)

## Sakatah Lake

Evidence: 5 Sites

[DNR camping inventory](https://maps.dnr.state.mn.us/cgi-bin/compass/feature_detail.cgi?id=spk00256)

## Remaining uncertainty

Minnesota Valley needs direct DNR confirmation of any operating individual hike-in inventory. Ann Lake has six walk-ins in the official map/older appendix, but its current overview omits the breakdown. No fixed count is assigned to dispersed camping. The NPS shoreline total is not represented as hike-in camping or retained as an unsupported 13. Count checks do not verify inherited hammock ratings.
