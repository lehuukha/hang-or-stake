# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Hammock and tent campers planning overnight trips around the Twin Cities, including the owner using a phone outdoors.

## Product Purpose
Find a suitable campsite, compare hammock and tent ratings, and inspect the source evidence before deciding.

## Brand Personality
Practical, candid, clear, with a high-end modern presentation. The owner authorized design judgment with clarity as the priority on September 6, 2026, then explicitly requested a more substantial high-end modern redesign.

## Anti-references
The previous oversized intro and schematic map, wall of area buttons, tiny labels and dense unbroken notes made the guide feel cluttered.

## Design Principles
- Put campsite finding before explanatory material.
- Reveal detail when requested, without discarding evidence.
- Preserve uncertainty, estimates and field report attribution.
- Use familiar controls and readable comparisons on a phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Design baseline: readable contrast, keyboard access, explicit form labels, adequate touch targets and reduced-motion support. No additional user-specific needs were supplied.
